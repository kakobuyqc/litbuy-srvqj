Litbuy spreadsheet guide

From browsing to a useful shortlist.

A simple process for keeping product research clear and repeatable.

01

Choose a focused category

Start with a specific need: a hoodie, a pair of shoes or a finishing accessory. Use the category directory to browse CAFANS, then save the exact product source rather than a vague search result.

02

Compare equivalent details

For Litbuy product links, record the selected size, color, material description and listed price. Keep currencies separate and date your notes so an older entry does not look like a current offer.

03

Verify before committing

Reopen the source listing. Check availability, seller information, measurements and return terms. Confirm whether the source link is currently supported by the service you plan to use.

Litbuy finds, considered carefully

Look beyond the thumbnail.

Fit is a measurement question.

For clothing, compare chest width, body length, shoulder width and sleeve length with a garment you already own. Record whether the seller measures an item laid flat or gives a body measurement.

For footwear, follow the specific chart and its measurement method. A familiar US, UK or EU label alone may not resolve differences between models.

A photo is one piece of evidence.

Product photos can help you inspect visible color, construction and details. They cannot independently establish authenticity, comfort, durability or the composition of a material.

Keep seller claims separate from verified information. If provenance matters to your decision, request appropriate documentation and assess the seller before buying.

Quick answers

Before you open
another product tab.

Direct answers to common questions about using a product-link spreadsheet and this independent guide.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product-link shortlist organized for shopping research. Useful fields include category, source URL, variant, listed price, measurements and personal notes. A spreadsheet is a research aid, not a guarantee of availability, authenticity or compatibility.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a shopping guide and category shortcuts to CAFANS. You can use its suggested fields to create your own spreadsheet.

Can every product link be used with Litbuy?

Do not assume universal compatibility. Check the current Litbuy interface and policies for the specific source link before placing an order.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Use the measurements on the specific listing. For clothing, compare chest, length, shoulder and sleeve measurements with a garment you own. For shoes, check the listing's size chart and measurement method rather than relying only on a regional size label.

Does a product image prove authenticity?

No. Product images and inspection photos do not independently establish authenticity. Verify the seller, provenance and supporting documentation before making a purchase decision.

Is this an official Litbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent editorial guide. Category and browsing links lead to CAFANS. Brand imagery is used as visual reference and does not establish affiliation or product availability.
